# Product overview

Locations is a private map journal for Google Timeline exports: heatmaps, day views with snapped journeys, day trips, and insights.

## Who it is for

People who exported Timeline from Google Takeout (or still can) and want a hosted, private explorer rather than a spreadsheet.

## What ships in the app

- **Hotspots:** visit density heatmap and named areas
- **Day View:** calendar, map, and timeline for one day
- **Day Trips:** long-range / multi-cluster days
- **Insights:** distance, modes, corridors, yearly/monthly stats
- **Settings:** sources (one Google account export each), account, billing, units

## What we store

Precise visit and activity points, inferred place names, travel modes, and account/billing metadata. See [Privacy](../legal/privacy.md).

## Demo

The public demo uses synthetic sample journeys on tenant `demo`. It cannot import or change sources.
