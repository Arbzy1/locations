# Accessibility statement

## Our commitment
We aim to make Locations usable to WCAG 2.1 Level AA and to evaluate relevant WCAG 2.2 improvements. This is a commitment to improvement, not a claim of certified conformance. European Accessibility Act and US accessibility obligations depend on the operator, service and applicable exemptions; no blanket exemption is asserted.

## Features and known limitations
The interface includes keyboard-focus indicators, labelled controls, show/hide password buttons, semantic dialogs, responsive layouts, light/dark themes and reduced-motion support. These features do not establish full compliance. Interactive maps, animated playback, complex charts, colour-coded modes and some dense controls may present barriers. Complete screen-reader, keyboard, contrast, zoom and mobile assistive-technology testing has not been completed. Text/list views and exports can assist access but do not reproduce every map interaction.

## Reporting a barrier
Contact the operator below with the page, task, assistive technology and preferred accessible response format. Do not include routes, coordinates or private location files. We will acknowledge the report within five working days and aim to provide an update or accessible alternative within 20 working days. If unresolved, request escalation to the operator. You may also contact your national accessibility enforcement body or equality authority; the relevant body depends on your residence and applicable law. UK advice is available from the Equality Advisory and Support Service at https://www.equalityadvisoryservice.com/.
