# Data Processing Agreement

## Scope and activation
This addendum is offered for an identified business controller and becomes binding only when the operator and business execute it with completed processing and transfer details. An individual account or tenant boundary alone does not establish a controller/processor relationship. Do not upload employee or other third-party location data before execution. The operator remains controller for its own account, billing and security purposes.

## Processing schedule
Subject matter is hosting private location history and Journey cost records for the controller. Duration is the agreed service term plus return/deletion. Nature and purpose include storage, organization, mapping, correction, calculation, export and deletion on documented instructions. Data includes identity/account references, timestamps, precise coordinates, activities, journeys, places, vehicles and user labels. Data subjects are only the authorized individuals specified by the controller in the signed schedule. The controller specifies any sensitive-data categories, lawful basis, Article 9 condition where needed, transparency and required employee consultation. No unrelated analytics, advertising or model training is authorized.

## Processor obligations
Process only on documented controller instructions, including instructions for international transfers, unless law requires otherwise; notify the controller before legally required processing unless prohibited. Notify the controller immediately if an instruction appears unlawful. Ensure authorized personnel are bound by confidentiality and least-privilege access. Maintain Article 32 measures appropriate to risk, including tenant authorization, FORCE RLS, restricted database roles, protected credentials, transport encryption, provider encryption at rest where verified, encrypted registration plates, and incident and recovery procedures. The executed schedule must record actual regions, backup lifetimes, access review and recovery testing; these are not certifications.

Assist the controller with rights requests, security, DPIAs, prior consultation and breach investigation taking account of available information and the processing. Forward rights requests promptly without independently responding except as instructed or legally required. Notify the controller of a personal-data breach without undue delay after awareness, with available facts and updates; do not wait for a complete investigation. The controller assesses its own 72-hour regulator deadline where applicable.

## Subprocessors and transfers
The executed schedule identifies authorized subprocessors using the Service providers list, their roles, regions and transfer safeguards. General written authorization requires at least 30 days' advance notice of additions or replacements and an opportunity to object on reasonable data-protection grounds. Resolve objections before the affected processing, or allow termination of the affected service. Impose equivalent Article 28 duties by contract and remain responsible for subprocessor performance. Transfers outside an adequate jurisdiction require the applicable SCCs, UK Addendum/IDTA, transfer assessment and supplementary measures. This page alone does not execute transfer clauses.

## Return, deletion and evidence
At the controller's choice, return or delete personal data at termination and delete copies unless law requires retention. Agree a written deletion deadline and backup expiry before signature; isolate retained backups and reapply deletion if restored. Make compliance information available and allow proportionate audits and inspections by the controller or its mandated auditor, including prompt cooperation with supervisory authorities. Maintain processing records and notify material security changes. The signed order identifies parties, contacts, instructions, TOMs and transfer annexes; Article 28 duties prevail over conflicting general terms.
