# Service providers

## Recipients and deployment details
The inventory below covers third-party integrations used by Locations, including optional services that run only when enabled on a deployment. Not all recipients act as data processors: payment, identity, public-authority, and mapping providers may act as independent controllers for parts of their processing under their own terms. Shared UK fuel prices, grid carbon intensity, and vehicle reference catalogues are synchronised without sending any user's Timeline history. No runtime generative AI, advertising SDK, or third-party client analytics SDK is used.

Active integrations depend on which optional features (such as Stripe billing, Google OAuth, Google Maps routing, or custom map tiles) are configured on this deployment. Business controllers entering into a Data Processing Agreement receive a signed subprocessor schedule and at least 30 days' advance notice of subprocessor changes.
